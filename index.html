About

As a dedicated neuroinformatics researcher, I am deeply passionate about unraveling the complexities of the brain. My work centers on developing bio-inspired spiking neural networks and implementing them on neuromorphic chips, aiming to create energy-efficient, intelligent machines that emulate the brain’s learning and adaptive capabilities. With a robust interdisciplinary background in physics and neuroscience, I bring a unique perspective to advancing the field of artificial intelligence, striving to make impactful contributions that bridge the gap between biological systems and technology.
